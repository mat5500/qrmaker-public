Common questions

What does Free include?
Every QR code type (links, Wi‑Fi, contacts, email, phone, SMS, location, events and text), Code 128 barcodes, unlimited scanning, PNG sharing and your latest 10 history items.
What does Pro add?
Colours, dot patterns, rounded eyes and your logo, every barcode format (EAN‑13, UPC‑A, EAN‑8, Code 39, PDF417, Aztec), PDF and SVG export, PNG up to 4096 px, printable batch label sheets and full history. It's a one-time purchase of €2.99, with no subscription.
I bought Pro on another device.
Open Settings › Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.
Do the codes expire?
No. QR Maker makes static codes that contain your content directly, so they keep working forever and never depend on our servers.
A code won't scan.
Keep strong contrast between code and background, leave a margin around it, and keep logos small. Print at least 2 cm wide for QR codes.
Is anything uploaded?
No. Everything happens on your device. See the privacy policy.
