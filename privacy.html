Privacy Policy

QR Maker: QR & Barcode · Last updated 24 October 2026
Short version: the codes you make and scan never leave your device. QR Maker has no account, no analytics and no advertising.

Your codes and history

Codes are created and read locally on your iPhone, iPad or Mac using Apple's built-in frameworks. What you type, what you scan and your history are stored only in the app on your device until you delete them. We never receive them.

Camera, photos and contacts

The camera is used only on the Scan screen, to read codes; video is never recorded or sent anywhere. Photos are read only when you pick one in the system photo picker, and the app saves to your photo library only when you choose Save. Contacts are accessed only when you choose to add a scanned contact card.

Opening scanned content

If you choose to open a scanned link, call a number, send an email or look up a product barcode, that action is handed to your browser or the matching app. Nothing is opened automatically.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details. To record and restore purchases we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives an anonymous app user identifier and the App Store transaction details (product, price, date, country). This information is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Data we do not collect

No contact details, no location, no contacts, no browsing history, no usage analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reports sent to us.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete codes from History (swipe, or Settings › Delete History) or delete the app. Purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat contain no personal content; contact us to ask about them.
